DATA SOURCES

Where displayed information comes from

Character and guild profile information comes from Blizzard’s World of Warcraft APIs and can lag behind the game. Run reports use deliberately published facts derived from recorded combat events. Tactics references are linked inside reports where available.

Artwork and game material

World of Warcraft names, imagery and game material are owned by Blizzard Entertainment. Artwork credits and external source links appear on relevant pages and reports.

Interpretation

Recorded events and source-backed mechanics are separated from coaching assessments. Where the available evidence cannot prove causation, the report should identify that uncertainty.
