SECURE BY DESIGN

What happens to a combat log

The secure service checks your account and file, rejects duplicates, applies upload limits and stores the raw log in private EU storage. You choose whether the finished report is public, party-only, private or unlisted, and the uploader can later change its visibility or request its removal.
