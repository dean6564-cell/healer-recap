Ranked by season score, or best recorded key when a dungeon is selected. Role is the character’s current specialization; it may differ from the role used in a run.

What this comparison includes

Blizzard’s available season profiles and best-run records. These are not a complete run history or a weekly activity count. A timed record is marked explicitly; unavailable data is never treated as a zero score.

Scores show progression, not individual combat performance. Deaths, missed interrupts, healing, damage and tactics assessments require combat logs. Score changes compare consecutive successful snapshots within the same season. Alts are listed as separate characters.

Refreshes query characters at the highest level present in the roster. Lower-level characters remain visible under “All guild characters”. A stale profile retains its earlier timestamp and is excluded from the current leaderboard.
